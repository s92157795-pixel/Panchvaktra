Panchvaktra Welfare Foundation: A social welfare initiative dedicated to supporting Hindu refugee families who left Pakistan and sought shelter in India. We aim to help families and children access essential educational, emotional, financial, social, and livelihood support so they can work toward a safer, more stable, and dignified future.

About Panchvaktra Welfare Foundation

Panchvaktra Welfare Foundation was established with a simple belief: every person deserves the opportunity to live with dignity, security, and hope.

Hindu families who left Pakistan and sought refuge in India may face a range of difficulties while trying to establish stable lives. Access to education, financial resources, employment, emotional support, and social acceptance can be important parts of this journey.

We recognized the need for a compassionate, community-oriented initiative that focuses not only on immediate needs but also on the opportunities that help individuals and families move toward long-term independence.

PROPkar (प्रोपकार)

The Spirit of Selfless Service

“सेवा में समर्पण, मानवता के लिए अर्पण।”

Meaning and purpose: Inspired by Paropkar (परोपकार), meaning helping others or acting for the welfare of others.

Propkar represents our commitment to serving humanity with compassion, kindness, and selflessness. Through this initiative, we aim to support vulnerable families, respond to community needs, and encourage a culture where helping others becomes a shared responsibility.

PRITI (प्रीति)

Love, Affection and Belonging

“अपनापन, स्नेह और सम्मान।”

Meaning and purpose: Priti means love, affection, warmth, and goodwill.

Priti reflects our belief that support goes beyond material assistance. We aim to foster emotional well-being, meaningful human connections, and social acceptance so that families feel welcomed, respected, and included in the communities they call home.

PRADAN (प्रदान)

The Act of Giving

“हर योगदान, किसी के जीवन में नई उम्मीद।”

Meaning and purpose: Pradan means giving, offering, or providing.

Pradan represents the spirit of sharing resources and extending meaningful assistance to those who need it. Through community contributions and responsible giving, we aim to help families access essential supplies, educational materials, and other forms of support according to their needs.

PRAYATNA (प्रयत्न)

Effort, Commitment and Perseverance

“हर प्रयास, बेहतर भविष्य की ओर।”

Meaning and purpose: If you intend this name to represent Prayatna (प्रयत्न), its meaning is effort, endeavour, or a sincere attempt.

Prayatna represents the spirit of sincere effort, determination, and perseverance in the pursuit of a better life. Through this initiative, Panchvaktra Welfare Foundation aims to empower refugee families by creating opportunities in education, skill development, employment, and personal growth.

PRAGATI (प्रगति)

Progress, Growth and Empowerment

“सशक्त जीवन, उज्ज्वल भविष्य।”

Meaning and purpose: Pragati means progress, advancement, or development.

Pragati represents our long-term vision of helping individuals and families build sustainable futures. By encouraging education, vocational skills, employment opportunities, and financial independence, we aspire to support lasting progress and greater participation in society.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential insights into our mission, humanitarian aid, child welfare, and community initiatives.

Q

What is the primary mission of Panchvaktra Foundation?

Panchvaktra is a humanitarian non-profit dedicated to providing shelter, nutrition, healthcare, and education to vulnerable and displaced children and families rebuilding their lives.

Q

How are donations and relief contributions utilized?

100% of all public contributions are deployed directly to on-ground children's welfare, free school supplies, daily nutrition, and emergency aid with strict financial auditing.

Q

Can individuals volunteer or mentor children with you?

Yes! We warmly welcome volunteer educators, healthcare workers, and community mentors. You can connect with our team through our contact channels to join our welfare network.

Q

How does the Foundation safeguard children's privacy?

We enforce zero-tolerance child-protection policies. All photos and stories are shared under guardian consent, protecting their safety, dignity, and future opportunities.

Q

Who does Panchvaktra Foundation primarily support?

We focus on displaced children and families from conflict-impacted regions (such as Pakistan and Afghanistan), as well as local impoverished communities needing shelter and care.

Q

How can someone in urgent need contact you for help?

Anyone seeking shelter, educational aid, or child welfare assistance can contact us via our WhatsApp hotline, community centers, or direct emergency messaging.
